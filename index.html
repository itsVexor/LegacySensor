Troubleshooting guide ↗
Do I need to create an account?+

No. Legacy Sensor works without a player account. Use the camera or connect your local phone WebApp and start playing.

What's different about Legacy Sensor Lite?+

Lite keeps Settings, Camera, Tracking, WebApp and Party Master. It leaves out Player Card, customization, rewards, Quiz Time and other extras, with a simplified interface designed for quicker startup.

How do I connect my phone?+

Connect the phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi, start Phone Sensor in the PC app and scan its QR code. Browser security prompts may depend on your local setup.

Is the tracking different in Lite?+

Lite is designed to keep the same core motion tracking as the Full edition. Real-world FPS depends on hardware and camera settings.

Which games are supported?+

Compatibility is build-dependent and experimental for some community ports. Check the compatibility section and the current release notes.

Why does my antivirus or VirusTotal flag Sensor.exe?+

Some older Legacy Sensor builds received malware-family detections, including “SilverFox” classifications. A detection alone does not establish whether the file is malicious or a false positive. A preliminary source review did not reveal obvious password- or cookie-stealing code in the examined sources, but that is not proof that a distributed EXE is safe. Always download from the project links, scan the exact file and compare its hash with a trusted release when available. Do not bypass a security warning just to run the app.

Has Legacy Sensor completed a full security audit?+

Not yet. Earlier source reviews identified sensitive local WebApp TLS-key placement and firewall-rule handling that require additional hardening. We do not claim the executable has been independently certified or that every antivirus alert is false. If you find a suspicious detection, share its file hash and a report through the community.

Do I need a Kinect to play?+

No. Legacy Sensor uses a webcam or supported camera source for motion tracking. A game-specific bridge may still be needed if you use a community PC game port expecting Kinect data.

What camera settings should I start with?+

Try 640 × 480 and 30 FPS with a well-lit area. If your PC is busy, lower preview rendering load before changing tracking accuracy. Your actual tracking FPS may be lower than camera FPS.

Is Legacy Sensor free?+

Legacy Sensor Full and Lite are free community beta builds. Download only from the project’s published release pages.

Can I play offline?+

Core webcam tracking and Party Master bots are designed to work locally. Phone pairing uses your local Wi-Fi network and browser; it does not require a player account.

Can I use a webcam and phone at the same time?+

The desktop app can use a supported camera source, while the separate Phone Sensor WebApp can pair over QR on your local network. Available slots and player behavior depend on the selected mode and game.

How do I install an older Linux release?+

Download a Linux ZIP from Older Versions, extract it, make the included launcher executable (for example with chmod +x) and start it on a supported Linux Mint / Ubuntu-based system. File names vary between releases.

Is it affiliated with Ubisoft or Just Dance?+

No. Legacy Sensor is an independent, unofficial fan project by ItsVexor and is not endorsed by Ubisoft or Just Dance.

Where can I report a bug or ask for help?+

Check the troubleshooting page and the community links above. For crashes, note your release version, Windows build, camera model and the last visible error. Never post private keys, passwords or unredacted pairing tokens.
